Windmill

Use Windmill on your computer from this phone. The AI keeps running on the computer.

1. Pick your computer

On the computer: open Windmill > Phone and switch on Let my phone connect. Make sure this phone is on the same Wi-Fi.

Looking for your computer…

Away from home, use the Tailscale address shown on the computer's Phone page.

2. Type the code

Type the 6-digit code shown on your computer's Phone page.

Can't see the computer's screen right now? Ask the computer instead: it shows a question you answer there once, when you're next at it.

Waiting for your computer

On your computer, Windmill asks whether this phone may connect. Click Allow there if it shows this number:

The question waits for 10 minutes. Wake the computer's screen if it's off.
